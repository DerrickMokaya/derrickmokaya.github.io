### 📊 Pension Sustainability & Demographic Risk Analysis
**Tools:** Excel, Statistical Modelling, Actuarial Methods  
**Context:** Academic/Internship – NSSF Kenya

Conducted a pension sustainability study analysing demographic trends and contribution vs. liability projections for a defined-benefit pension scheme. Applied actuarial assumptions including mortality tables, discount rates, and salary growth projections to assess long-term fund viability.

**Key outputs:** Funding ratio projections, sensitivity analysis, executive summary report.

**Context:** Finance Intern – NSSF Kenya

**Key outputs:** Automated Excel reconciliation tool, data quality report.
